# Vehkavaraus user manual

> **Note:** This documentation was generated by Claude Opus 5.5 (Anthropic) based on the project source code.

Vehkavaraus ("Vehkapolku's Rent-A-Game") lets tenants of Vehkapolku borrow the building's shared board games. Use the website to see which games are available, mark a game as borrowed when you take it, and mark it returned when you bring it back.

The site is at [vehkavaraus.joovil.dev](https://vehkavaraus.joovil.dev).

## Getting started

### Creating an account

1. Click **Sign up** in the top right corner.
2. Fill in the form:

   | Field            | What to enter                                                                     |
   | ---------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
   | Username         | At least 3 characters. You will use this to log in.                               |
   | Password         | A password of your choice.                                                        |
   | Confirm Password | The same password again.                                                          |
   | Email            | A working email address. The verification link is sent here.                     |
   | Apartment        | Your apartment: the letter **A** or **B** followed by the number, e.g. `A12` or `B3`. |

   The Email and Apartment fields turn red until the value is in a valid format.

3. Click **Sign up**. You are taken to the **Log in** page.

### Verifying your email

You must verify your email address before you can borrow games.

1. Open the email titled **"Verify your email"** from `noreply@vehkavaraus.joovil.dev`.
2. Click the **Verify your email** button in the email.
3. The page shows **"Account verified"**. After 5 seconds you are taken onwards automatically.

If the email doesn't arrive, log in and click **Resend verification** below the top bar. A new email is sent. Use the link in the newest email; older links stop working.

### Logging in and out

- **Log in:** click **Log in**, enter your username and password, and click **Log in**. You are taken to the games page.
- **Log out:** click **Log out** in the top bar, then **Log out** again to confirm (or **Cancel**).

## Navigating the site

The top bar contains:

- **Vehkapolku's Rent-A-Game**: goes to the games page.
- **Your username**: goes to your user page, where you see your borrowed games and settings.
- **Log in / Sign up** when logged out, or **Log out** when logged in.

## Borrowing a game

1. Open the games page by clicking **Vehkapolku's Rent-A-Game** in the top bar.
2. Find a game with a green **Borrow** button. These games are available.
3. Click **Borrow**, then **Confirm**. Click **Cancel** if you change your mind.
4. The card now shows **Available: day/month**, the date the game is due back. Pick up the game.

Good to know:

- The loan period is **one week**.
- Games that are already borrowed show **Available:** followed by the date they are due back, e.g. `Available: 17/10` means 17 October.
- You can borrow several games at the same time.

## Your user page

Click your username in the top bar to open your user page.

### Returning a game

Your user page lists each game you have borrowed and its **due date**.

1. Bring the game back to its place.
2. Click **Return Game** next to the game. The game is removed from your list and becomes available to others.

### Settings

- **Update password:** enter the new password in **Password** and **Password again**, then click **Submit**. You see "Password changed. Please log back in". After a few seconds you are logged out; log in again with the new password.
- **Update apartment:** enter the new apartment (e.g. `A12`) in **Apartment** and **Apartment again**, then click **Submit**. You see "Apartment updated".

The two fields must match, and the **Submit** button stays disabled until they do.

## For administrators

Administrators manage the game collection and keep track of borrows. Admin rights are granted by the site maintainer.

### Opening the admin panel

When logged in as an administrator, click the **logo** in the top-left corner, or go to [vehkavaraus.joovil.dev/admin/admin-panel](https://vehkavaraus.joovil.dev/admin/admin-panel).

### The games table

The table lists all games. On a wide screen it shows these columns:

| Column      | Meaning                                    |
| ----------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| Name        | Game name. Click to open its details.      |
| Status      | Free, Borrowed or Late (see colours below) |
| Apartment   | Apartment of the current borrower          |
| Borrow date | When the game was borrowed                 |
| Due date    | When the game should be returned           |

Status colours:

- **Free** (green): the game is available.
- **Borrowed** (orange): the game is borrowed and not yet due.
- **Late** (pink): the due date has passed. Games are marked late automatically once a day.

### Game details

Click a game's name to open its details. Click the name again, or **X**, to close them. The details show:

- the game's image and status
- for borrowed games: the borrower's apartment, the borrow date and the due date
- **History**: earlier borrows of the game, newest first

Actions:

- **Mark as returned**: marks the current borrow as returned and makes the game available. Use this when a tenant has brought the game back but forgot to return it on the site.
- **Delete game**: removes the game and its image. Click **Confirm** to delete, or **Cancel** to keep it.

### Adding a game

The **New game** form is at the bottom of the admin panel. It is visible when no game's details are open.

1. Type the game's name in **Name**.
2. Click **Select image** and choose a photo of the game. On a computer you can also drag the image onto **Drop image here**. Images should be square.
3. Check the image preview.
4. Click **Add game**. The message "Game added successfully" appears, and the game shows up in the table and on the games page.

## FAQ

**How long can I keep a game?**
One week from the day you borrow it. The due date is shown on your user page.

**Can I borrow more than one game?**
Yes.

**I borrowed a game by mistake. What do I do?**
Go to your user page and click **Return Game** next to it.

**I moved to another apartment. How do I update it?**
Open your user page and use **Update apartment** under Settings.

**Who do I contact if something doesn't work?**
Contact the site administrator.
